**Project:** InfinitoComics Web

**Purpose:** This document lists all images required across the Career, Internships, About Us, Founder Profile, and Research pages — with recommended sizes, aspect ratios, and usage notes.

---

## General Rules (Apply to All Pages)

- Save all images as **WebP** format for best performance (fallback: JPG/PNG)
- Compress every image to **under 200 KB** where possible
- Banner/hero images may go up to 500 KB given their size
- All circle-cropped team/avatar photos must be **square (1:1)** with the face centered
- All full-width banners must be **landscape** (wider than tall)
- Portrait photos of people should be **3:4** (vertical)
